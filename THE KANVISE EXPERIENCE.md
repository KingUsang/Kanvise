# **THE KANVISE EXPERIENCE** 

The Kanvise Experience matters just as much as the solution or product we are building. We are able to successfully map out the difference between a tutorial on KANVISE and a tutorial that is not. 

Tutorial’s Experience; 

1. Downloadable Reports : The tutorials should be able to download a performance reports on their students and tutors 

2. Weekly Updates: They should receive weekly updates on classes, tutors and students at risk, number of new students that joined, the overall performance of students 

3. Seamless Registration; More than anything else, they should be able to send a link through their marketing funnel,  students are able to make payments, get registered, students phone numbers can automatically be added to their ‘contacts.google’ so they can still add them to the class group chat on whatsapp, get their money into their accounts easily and on the same day. 

4. Recordable classes; Tutorials should be able to record their classes so students absent can still access it. 

5. Tutorials should be able to upload their syllabus so that their progress can be well monitored. 

## Tutor’s Experience: 

1. AI QUIZ AUTOMATION: Tutors should get automated questions as they are teaching for students to attempt, to confirm if they truly understand and to confirm their presence on the call. These questions should be suited to the exam and subjects the students are prepping for. 

2. Upload files on the white board: The use of the whiteboard should come with zero hassle, the mobile interface should also be great, it is very important that KANVISE supports the mobile experience without crashing. 

3. Mock breakdown system: Tutors should be able to set up mocks that don't just give overview results but diagnostic reports of every student, what they scored in every subject, the topics they failed, their average speed per question, the questions they spent more time on. 

4. Easy assignment upload and grading; Tutors shouldn't have to download every assignment, they should be able to view and grade on the website and give grades and remarks. 

5. At the end of every class, they should get their annotations for their materials just in case they want to use it in another class. 

6. Based off the syllabus and the time of the programme, tutors know if they need to move faster or not. 

## THE STUDENT’S EXPERIENCE 

1. The website should be mobile and data friendly for students. It matters a lot that they don't spend heavily on data just to learn. They are just students and some of them are bearing the cost of the tutorial already. 

2. The students should be able to upload assignments, take mocks, watch recorded classes when absent. 

3. The students upon registration should be sent a digital copy of their programme schedule with ‘powered by kanvise’ and the name of the tutorial on the schedule. 

4. Students should be alerted by email,phone,push notification etc in the morning of the class, afternoon before the class, and every hour when they have about six hours to the class using the Team mascot. 

5. Immediately the class starts and they are still yet to join they should be alerted and the gamification should be used to guilt trip them. 

6. They should get access to recorded classes, they should have easy access to their performance report and where and how they can be better. 

## GAMIFICATION FOR THE STUDENT’S INTERFACE 

01. Their profile pictures should be animated, they get to choose 

which mascot they want to be and their profile names should be names they imputed during registration 

02. XP system- They get to earn points from attendance,assignment submission,mock completion, answers during classes. 

03. Levels/ Ranks - This will be based on subject mastery e.g Chemistry-ACE. This is for each tutorial and not a global competition. The levels/ranks will be based on how well they perform in their mocks 

04. Progress Bar- the percentage of their syllabus completed per subject for the program 

05. Skill Tree - The syllabus can be laid out visually unlocking sequentially as prerequisites are completed 

06. Daily streaks - This is marked by their daily attendance to class 

07. Daily Quests- 2-3 small goals shown on the dashboard e.g Submit an assignment before deadline, attempt a mock etc 

08. Loss-aversion notifications — midday/evening nudges 

before streak expires, comeback message after a miss (tone: playful/peer-pressure) 

09. Leagues — weekly cohort brackets (Bronze → Silver → Gold) scoped within a tutorial 

10. Cohort leaderboard — class-scoped, not national 

11. Peer nudges — "Chidi just overtook you in Chemistry this week" 

12. Social status flair — public profile title/badge visible to classmates 

13. Badges — milestone-based ("5 mocks in a row," "Under 30 mins," "First 300+") 

14. Milestone celebrations — full-screen animation at streak/score milestones 

15. Hearts/lives — after 3 wrong answers, forced quick review of that topic before continuing 

16. Boss battles — harder "final mock" per topic before next topic unlocks 

17. Unlockables — new topics/mock sets gated behind prerequisite completion 

18. Tutor-set challenges — custom weekly goals tutor assigns to their own cohort 

19. At-risk digest — weekly list to tutor of students about to lose streaks, so tutor can personally nudge 

20. Weekly "Most Improved" spotlight — tutor shouts out live in class
