# Team

Route: `/team`

The Team page lists organization administrators and exposes a Remove action for removable members. The Add team member dialog accepts optional first name, required last name, and required email; its copy warns that team members can manage everything and can be assigned as a client manager. Submission sends an invitation. The artifact captures blank dialog, filled form, and the created synthetic member’s pending-list state.

The Remove confirmation was also captured without submission. Its copy explains that removal revokes an unaccepted invitation and can delete the person's account and personal data when they belong to no other Bobbin organization.
