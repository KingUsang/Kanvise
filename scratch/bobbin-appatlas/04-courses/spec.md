# Courses

Routes: `/courses`, `/courses/:id`, `/courses/:id/outline`, `/courses/:id/lessons`

## List and sample course

The Courses list separates a seeded sample course from the organization’s real course list. The sample detail contains Summary, Course outline, Lessons, Resources, Homework, and About surfaces; its summary presents plan coverage, lesson history, and roster cards.

## New course wizard

1. **Name and presentation**: required name, optional description, colour, and delivery context.
2. **People**: independent selection of eligible students and tutors, plus add-new shortcuts.
3. **Review & create**: explicit review of name, description, students, and tutors.

The submitted synthetic course exposes a detail surface with Actions, New lesson, Summary, Lessons, Resources, Homework, About, roster remove controls, and course-plan paths (browse plans or describe a course for AI drafting).
