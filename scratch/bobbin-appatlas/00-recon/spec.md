# Recon and setup

The account began on a five-step setup journey: client, tutor, student, course, and first lesson. After the synthetic client, tutor, student, course, and lesson were created, `/setup` resolved to the ordinary dashboard rather than an onboarding checklist, confirming setup completion.

The dashboard includes Today, This week, Needs attention, sample-course discovery, classroom-guidance content, a mobile-app prompt, trial/billing prompt, and a live-view/calendar route into scheduled lessons.
