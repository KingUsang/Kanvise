## 1. Who We Are

Kanvise ("Kanvise", "we", "us", "our") is an online platform that lets tutorial centres and private tutors run a digital school: live classes, notes, assignments, mock exams, attendance, and payments. Kanvise is operated by its founding team at https://kanvise.com.

**Contact for privacy matters:** privacy@kanvise.com

This policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal data, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the data protection laws that apply to our users, which may include the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and other applicable laws.

## 2. Our Role: Controller and Processor

Kanvise has two roles, depending on the data:

- **Kanvise as data controller.** For your account, your use of the platform, our subscription billing with tutorial centres, our marketing, and platform security, we decide how and why your data is used.
- **Kanvise as data processor.** A tutorial centre ("Centre") that uses Kanvise decides what student and tutor data to put on the platform and why (for example, its student records, class content, grades, and fee settings). For that data, the Centre is the controller and Kanvise processes it on the Centre's behalf. Questions about how a Centre uses your data, or requests about records a Centre holds, should go to that Centre first. We will help the Centre respond.

## 3. Who This Policy Covers

- **Admins:** owners or managers of a Centre.
- **Tutors:** teachers invited by a Centre.
- **Students:** people enrolled in a Centre's programmes or courses.
- **Visitors:** people browsing kanvise.com or a Centre's public page without an account.

## 4. Data We Collect

### 4.1 Data you give us

| Category | Examples | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | First and last name, email, password (handled by our authentication provider; we never see or store your password in readable form), role, Kanvise user ID (e.g. KNV-STU-00042) | All users |
| Profile data | Bio, profile photo (tutors), avatar customisation choices (skin tone, hair, outfit, accessories), chosen mascot, display name | All users |
| Contact data | Phone number, WhatsApp number | Students and Centres, where provided |
| Centre data | Centre name, logo, banner, description, video intro, contact details, social links, bank details for payouts | Admins |
| Learning content | Notes, assignment files, student submissions, mock exam answers (MCQ and theory), syllabus uploads | Tutors and students |
| Feedback | Reviews and ratings, grades, tutor comments | Students and tutors |
| Messages | In-class chat messages | Live class participants |

### 4.2 Data from Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign up or log in with Google, Google shares the following with us, based on the permissions you approve:

- Your name
- Your email address (and whether it is verified)
- Your Google profile picture
- A unique Google account identifier

We use this information only to create and secure your Kanvise account, sign you in, and show your name and picture in your profile. We do not receive your Google password. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, calendar, or other Google data through sign-in. You can stop using Google sign-in or revoke Kanvise's access at any time in your Google Account settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

**Google API Services User Data Policy.** Kanvise's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security purposes, to comply with law, or for internal operations as permitted by that policy.

### 4.3 Data created when you use Kanvise

- **Attendance data:** when you join or leave a live class, and how long you stayed.
- **Performance data:** mock scores, time taken per question, topics missed, assignment scores, and diagnostic reports.
- **Engagement and gamification data:** XP, levels and ranks, streaks, badges, daily quests, league positions, and leaderboard standings within your Centre.
- **Notification data:** in-app, email, and (where enabled) push notification records and read status.
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser and device type, operating system, log data, timestamps, and security events.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see Section 12.

### 4.4 Live class audio, video, and recordings

During live classes, your audio and video (if your camera and microphone are on) are transmitted in real time. If your camera is off, your avatar is shown instead. When a Centre or tutor records a class, the recording captures tutor video, shared board, documents, annotations, and class audio, and may include students' voices and any student video or chat shown. Recordings are stored so enrolled students who missed the class can watch later. Participants are informed when recording is on.

### 4.5 Payment data

Payments are processed by **Paystack**. We do not receive or store your full card number, CVV, or bank login details. We receive and store payment records: amount, fee split, transaction reference, status, date, and what was purchased. Centres also provide bank account details to set up a Paystack subaccount for payouts.

### 4.6 Data from other sources

- **Your Centre** may add or import student or tutor details.
- **Our payment provider** confirms whether a payment succeeded.
- **Google Contacts sync (optional, Centre-controlled).** Only if a Centre turns this on and grants permission through its own Google account, student names and phone numbers may be added to that Centre's Google Contacts so the Centre can add students to a class group chat. We use that permission only for this purpose and only on the Centre's instruction.

**We do not sell your personal data.**

## 6. Who We Share Data With

**Within your Centre.**
- *Admins* can see user lists, payments, attendance, and results for their school.
- *Tutors* can see data for the courses they teach (attendance for their classes, submissions to their assignments, results for their mocks).
- *Students* can see their own data. Gamification features such as leaderboards and leagues may show your display name, mascot, and rank to classmates in the same Centre only.

**Public pages.** A Centre's public page may show tutor names, photos, bios, subjects, enrolled student counts, and published reviews (reviewer name as displayed). Do not put information in a public bio or review that you do not want visible to anyone.

**Service providers (processors).** We use trusted providers to run Kanvise. They process data only on our instructions and under data-protection terms:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Database and authentication |
| Google | Sign in with Google; optional AI features (Gemini API); optional Contacts sync if a Centre enables it |

**Legal and safety.** We may disclose data to regulators, courts, law enforcement, or others when required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

**Business transfers.** If Kanvise is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal data may transfer to the new owner, who must honour this policy.

Where we transfer personal data across borders, we do so in line with applicable law, using safeguards such as contractual data-protection terms (for example, standard contractual clauses where required). Contact us for details of the safeguards used.

## 8. How Long We Keep Data

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and profile data | While your account is active, then deleted or anonymised within 90 days of closure, except where we must keep it by law |
| Learning content, submissions, grades, attendance | While the Centre's account is active; preserved if a user is removed from a school so the Centre's historical records stay intact; deleted or anonymised within 90 days after the Centre closes its account |
| Class recordings | Until the Centre deletes them, or 12 months after the Centre's subscription ends |
| Payment records | As long as required for tax and accounting law (typically several years) |
| Technical and security logs | Up to 12 months |
| Marketing contacts | Until you opt out, or 24 months of inactivity |

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it so it can no longer identify you.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how your data is used (this policy).
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data (you can edit most profile data yourself).
- **Delete** your data, subject to legal and legitimate retention needs.
- **Restrict** or **object** to certain processing, including direct marketing.
- **Data portability:** receive your data in a commonly used format where applicable.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time where processing relies on consent.
- **Not be subject to solely automated decisions** with legal or similarly significant effects. MCQ auto-grading is a scoring function, and theory answers are reviewed by tutors.
- **Complain** to your local data protection authority (for example, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or your national supervisory authority in the EU/UK) if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise a right, email privacy@kanvise.com. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days. If your data is held by a Centre as controller, we may direct your request to the Centre or help the Centre respond.

## 10. Children and Students Under 18

Many students are teenagers, and Kanvise is built for Centres whose students may be minors.

- Where a user is under 18 (or under the age of digital consent in their country), the Centre is responsible for obtaining any parent or guardian consent required by law before enrolling them.
- If we learn that we hold a child's data without required consent, we will take steps to delete it or obtain consent.
- Parents and guardians may contact the Centre or us at privacy@kanvise.com to ask about, correct, or delete a child's data.
- We do not knowingly use children's data for third-party advertising.

## 11. Security

We protect personal data with measures that include:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic.
- Strict separation of each Centre's data, enforced on every request.
- Role-based access, so users see only what their role and enrolment allow.
- Short-lived signed links for private files.
- Verified payment and live-class webhooks.
- Passwords managed by a dedicated authentication provider, with minimum strength rules.
- Rate limiting and access controls on our servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to risk your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant authority and affected individuals within the time required by applicable law (for example, 72 hours under the NDPA and GDPR).

## 12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use:

- **Essential cookies** to keep you signed in (secure, HttpOnly session cookies) and to protect the platform. These are required for Kanvise to work.
- **Preference storage** to remember simple settings.

We do not currently use advertising cookies. You can control cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop you from signing in.

## 13. Notifications and Marketing Messages

Gamified reminders and nudges are intended to help you attend and stay on track; you can opt out of non-essential nudges by contacting us at privacy@kanvise.com. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe link.

## 14. Third-Party Links

Centre public pages and the platform may link to external sites (such as a Centre's website or social profiles). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
